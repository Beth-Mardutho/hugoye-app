    SYRIAC-SCRIPT INSCRIPTIONS AND GRAFFITI AT THE MONASTERY OF ST. ANTONY (EGYPT)

    AARON MICHAEL BUTTS

    UNIVERSITY OF HAMBURG

    ABSTRACT

    In this article, I present Syriac and Garshuni inscriptions and graffiti from the Monastery of St. Antony in the Egyptian desert along the Red Sea. These Syriac-script writings serve as evidence for Syriac Christians at this monastery, establishing their presence in the late sixteenth century and thereby supplementing what was previously known about earlier centuries from several notes found in Syriac and Garshuni manuscripts.
